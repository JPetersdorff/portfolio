# Chatbot Test Questions

100 questions a curious site visitor might ask the portfolio chatbot about *you*. Grouped by theme, written independent of any specific system prompt/persona — use this to test content and answer quality, then tune the system prompt separately.

Checkboxes are there so you can tick off ones you've tested and note gaps in the chatbot's answers.

## Background & Career Journey
- [ ] What's your career background before you started learning to code?
- [ ] Why did you decide to move from data science into software/web development?
- [ ] What were you doing professionally before tech?
- [ ] How long have you been coding?
- [ ] What sparked the idea to build a portfolio site with a chatbot?
- [ ] Did you study computer science, or are you self-taught?
- [ ] What's the biggest career pivot you've made?
- [ ] Where are you based, and are you open to relocating?
- [ ] What industries have you worked in?
- [ ] What does a typical workday look like for you right now?

## Technical Skills & Stack

- [ ] How would you rate your Python skills?
- [ ] What's your experience level with React?
